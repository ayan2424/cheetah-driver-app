Privacy Policy & Compliance

Cheetah Driver & Picker Mobile Suite

Effective Date: September 8, 2026 • Version: v1.0.0 Commercial

This Privacy Policy outlines how the Cheetah Driver & Warehouse Picker Mobile Application ("the Application"), developed by Cheetah Logistics, handles the collection, processing, transmission, and protection of user data for delivery couriers and warehouse fulfillment staff.

1. Location Data (Foreground & Background Telemetry)

Why Location Data is Required:

  • Navigation: Provides one-tap turn-by-turn routing via Google Maps, Apple Maps, and Waze to customer delivery addresses.
  • Live Dispatcher Tracking: Broadcasts periodic GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, speed, and heading) to central logistics dispatchers while shipments are marked "Out for Delivery".
  • ePOD Geostamping: Records legal delivery location coordinates on consignee Proof of Delivery (ePOD) receipts.

Background Location: Background tracking occurs only during active driver shifts when assigned parcels are en route. A persistent notification is displayed in the Android status bar whenever tracking is active. Drivers may disable tracking by logging out.

2. Camera & Proof of Delivery (ePOD) Media

  • Barcode & QR Scanning: The device camera is accessed to scan parcel tracking numbers (CHT- format) and warehouse bin shelf barcodes. No video or camera streams are permanently stored on device.
  • Drop-off Proof Photos: Photos taken upon parcel delivery are compressed, watermarked with tracking details on the server, and transmitted securely via HTTPS.
  • Touchscreen Signatures: Recipient signatures are exported as PNG graphics and attached to the digital delivery receipt.

3. Security & Data Storage

All network traffic is encrypted via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS. Authentication credentials and offline delivery records are protected with AES-256 hardware-backed encryption using the Android Keystore and Apple iOS Keychain.

4. Zero Advertising Policy

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade driver locations, customer information, or package telemetry to third-party ad networks or brokers. Data is strictly utilized for commercial logistics operations.
